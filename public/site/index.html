Questions

Good to know.

Is Timelysa free for customers?+

Browsing and booking in the app is simple and straightforward. Any service price is set by the business and shown before you book.

How does check-in work?+

Each booking has a QR pass and a reference. The business scans it, sees your booking and marks you as arrived. No camera? They can type the reference.

Can I cancel or change a booking?+

Yes — open your upcoming bookings to cancel or rebook. Businesses see the change straight away.

How does a business join?+

Send your details, the Timelysa team confirms with you on WhatsApp or email, creates your login, and you add services, hours and a photo to start taking bookings.

Which areas are covered?+

Timelysa is growing across South Africa, starting with local businesses in Gauteng. Get in touch to bring yours on board.
